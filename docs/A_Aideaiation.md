I want to build an app that combines Security, safety and risk management modules.
The app must have a backend displaying all the summary information of statistics collected from incidents, risks and guard patrols on a dashboard.
1. Security module must account for incident recording and management.  It must be able to create a Customer.  A customer will have Sites with geo location assigned to it.  Each site have Checkpoints also with geo location assigned to it.  The Checkpoints are QR Codes to be scanned by guards when performing their patrols,.  A series of checkpoints must be assigned a schedule. A checkpoint appears Red on the GPS map, when not yet scanned within its defined schedule and green when scanned within its schedule. A Schedule has to be assigned to a guard to conduct the scanning of the checkpoints.  A guard or guards must be assigned a Schedule in order to conduct the patrols on an hourly, two hourly or defined time band. An automatic exception notification in an email or Telegram notification must be generated when a guard misses a checkpoint or a checkpoints missed as per a defined Schedule. The user must be able to schedule a daily, weekly or monthly auto generated reports based on the patrols and the report is emailed to multiple email addresses per the defined time to run report.

*2. Risk Management module* works with a mobile app.  The mobile app records a risk.  The risk is defined into three categories.  A health risk, a safety risk or security risk.  The risk is further categorised into Impact and likelihood.  The impact can be either High, Medium and Low.  The likelihood is also categorised into High, Medium and Low. A photo graph has to be associated with a risk.  An approximate cost has to be associated with each risk that is recorded on the mobile app.  The mobile app must be able to record the GPS coordinates of where the risk was recorded. At the backend, the dashboard must show a highlevel summary of the main categories of risk - Show a count of risk per category. A sum of the values per category.  Number of escalated risk due to non-action within the defined turn around to address the risk.  Risks with High Impact and High Likelihood requires action or update of the risk within 24 hours.  Same as Medium Impact and High likelihood within 48 hours.  The notification for escalation based on defined times is emailed or Telegram.  Each escalation notificaiton must contain the full details of the risk, and Google map link to where the risk was initially reported.  An auto generated daily detailed report, weekly summary report and monthly highlevel report of the risks must be able to be automatically emailed as per defined time schedule.

*3.  Incident management module.*   Incidents are recorded either on a mobile app or desktop.  Recording of mobile app must include a GPS Coordinate of where the incident occured.  There must be a descrption field, with Severity of the incident from High, Medium and Low.  Every High incident must trigger a notification by email and telegram to defined people.  High incidents require an action or comment to update the incident within 24 hours. Failing to update the incident must trigger a notificaition to various people.  This is also known as escalation process.  Same applies to medium indicents that require a 48 hours turn around time.  A daily auto report is required for all incidents.  A weekly summary report has to be generated showing summary of all the incidents within a week.  A monthly auto report must be generated for all incidents reported within the month.
