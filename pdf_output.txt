 1.  Executive Summary  
The SCPNG Corporate Plan 2026 ΓÇô2028 translates the CommissionΓÇÖs long -term strategic vision into actionable 
priorities for the next three years. It provides a structured framework of programs, initiatives, and measurable 
performance indicators to deliver SCP NGΓÇÖs mandate of regulating Papua New GuineaΓÇÖs capital markets.  
 
SCPNG plays a pivotal role in economic development by safeguarding market integrity, protecting investors, and 
promoting financial inclusion. This plan outlines strategic priorities that will update  the legislative framework of 
the PNG capital markets to expand market opportunities, ensure compliance with the legislation and rules, and 
enhance institutional capacity by strengthening leadership and governance to meet the evolving needs of the 
capital market.  
 
The plan is structured around four Strategic Goals , each supported by Key Result Areas (KRAs):  
ΓÇó Strategic Goal 1:  Policy Development -Update the PNG capital market legislative framework  to and 
encourage additional products in the market.  
ΓÇó Strategic Goal 2:  Enforcement ΓÇô Licencing and supervision of the legislation and rules   
ΓÇó Strategic Goal 3:  Education - Empower Investors , stakeholders and service providers through education 
and Awareness  
ΓÇó Strategic Goal 4: Enhance Institutional Performance -Improve capability, capacity and services by the 
SCPNG.  
Together, these goals encompass 20 KRAs , each with defined objectives, initiatives, and performance targets. By 
2028, SCPNG aims to achieve increased investor participation  through the introduction of innovative financial 
instruments facilitated by an updated legislative framework , improved compliance  through active enforcement 
and monitoring , educated investors and regulated  service providers and enhanced institutional performance.  
 
This plan is fully aligned with Vision 2050 , the Medium -Term Development Plan IV (MTDP IV) , and the Capital 
Market Development Strategy . Implementation of this Corporate Plan will ensur e that SCPNGΓÇÖs efforts contribute 
meaningfully to national development priorities.  
2.  Legal Mandate  of SCP NG 
The Securities Commission of Papua New Guinea (SCPNG) is established under the Securities Commission Act  
2015  and is responsible for administering and enforcing the Capital Market Act 2015  and other relevant 
legislation. Its mandate includes:  
ΓÇó Advising the Minister on capital market policy and legislative reforms.  
ΓÇó Regulating and monitoring capital market activities and participants.

4.  Strategic Context  
SCPNG operates in a dynamic environment shaped by a need to update the legislative framework and develop 
internal capabilities . The national development frameworks  and strong government support provide a clear 
sense of direction, and  enthusiastic staff provide a solid base for development.  
There is demand in the market for thematic bonds, SME financing, regional integration, and digital innovation. 
Opening the market to realise these demands requires an update of the legislative framework. The SCPNG has 
an opportunity to develop its institutional capability and lift the performance of its outdated ICT infrastructure.  
The PNG Capital markets face threats from  global market volatility, cybersecurity risks, and resistance to 
regulatory reforms , which  must be proactively managed.

5.1.  Strategic  Goal 1: Policy Development   
The capital market regulatory environment suits the PNG economy, provides clear rules about appropriate 
behavior, and encourages additional products into the market.  
5.1.1.   Legislative  Framework  
The capital market legislation requires updating to ensure that it better suits the PNG economy and market 
environment . The goal is to introduce modern capital markets legislation which  takes account of PNG economic 
conditions and allows  a broader range of products to suit SME and include thematic bonds. The first step in this 
process is to d efine the policy  framework . Without the policy framework, it is not possible to draft the legislation . 
For the first period, the SCPNG will be developing the policy frame work  that will provide the basis for draft ing 
the legislation.  
5.1.2.  Regulations, codes, ands guidelines required to support the legislation.  
The CMA and CDA provide for regulations and codes which set out detailed rules for how capital markets will 
operate. These regulations, codes and guidelines provide certainty and clarity for market participants. The SCPNG 
will prioritise this work to focus on outstanding matters which are required to facilitate  the smooth operation of 
the PNG Capital Markets.

5.2.  Strategic Goal 2: Enforcement  and Compli ance  
Capital market participants are appropriately vetted, comply with the law and rules of the market, and 
appropriate sanctions are applied when participants breach the law so that investors are protected.  
5.2.1.  Licencing  
The application process will be reviewed with a view to allowing on -line applications to streamline the process 
and reduce the time taken. Internal manuals and documentation setting out the SCPNGΓÇÖs processes and how it 
applies appropriate criteria will be reviewed and updated.  
The SCPNG will develop appropriate conditions to be included on licences. These conditions will resolve 
uncertainty about expected standards of performance by operators to ensure investors are protected .  
5.2.2.  Supervision  
The SCPNG has developed its programme to monitor and supervise market activity to identify situations where 
behaviour or circumstances might impair the orderly functioning of the market which, if left unchecked, could 
undermine the confidence of investors in the market.  
The monitoring programme will comprise a mix of scheduled and unscheduled reviews of Licence holders  and 
unit trust licence holders . 
5.2.3.  Investigations  
The SCPNG  has been developing its investigation capability and capacity.  It will become more active in 
conducting formal enforcement investigations where it identifies behaviour likely to be in breach of the 
legislation . 
 
The SCPNG will develop an Investigation manual which sets out a range of matters such as investigation planning, 
processes, and handling documentary evidence.

6.  Risk Management  
Risk management is the process of identifying, assessing, and mitigating potential events or conditions that could 
negatively impact the achievement of organizational objectives. It ensures that SCPNG can anticipate challenges 
and respond proactively to mi nimize disruption and safeguard its reputation, resources, and strategic outcomes.  
Below is a summary of key risks identified for the implementation of this Corporate Plan.  
Risk Type  Likelihood  Impact  Mitigation Strategy  
Budget Risk  Medium  High Engage Treasury and seek donor support  
Capacity risk  High  Medium  Recruitment and training  
Reputational Risk  Low  High  Stakeholder Engagement  
7.  Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E)  
Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E) is a systematic process used to track progress, assess performance, and ensure 
accountability in the implementation of the Corporate Plan. It helps SCPNG measure results against planned 
objectives and KPIs, identify areas fo r improvement, and inform future planning.
